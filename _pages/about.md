Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he is a member of the HKUST NLP Group. He is advised by Professor Junxian He. He received his B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where he was awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. His research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
